# How it works

## The first cohort

**Five people.** Ethan runs the intake alone and the evaluation is solely his, then he brings
stats and a recommendation back to the group for a joint decision.
*Agreed 2026-09-24. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-24.md`*

**St Aloysius first.** Autonomous, flexible, faculty the group trusts, and lecturers who already
run companies. SJEC does not have that flexibility. The dean of fellowships has named 50 students
described as the cream of the crop; Ethan is sceptical of that selection and will run it anyway.
*Agreed 2026-09-17, cohort source confirmed 2026-09-24.
→ `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**An experiment on known people with known ideas.** The first cohort is lecturers and their
companies rather than students arriving with their own ideas.
*Framed by Arjun 2026-09-17. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**Six months.** Long enough for a company to get a real read on product-market fit.
*Advised by Shobith 2026-09-17. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

## What the programme actually does

**Distribution before funding.** Any student picked will know how to build a product. The two
blockers are funds and distribution, and distribution is the harder one. Get them their first five
leads, or even one or two.

The long game: FPO's own name becomes the distribution, the way YC's does. That means two tracks
running at once, the cohort and FPO's own credibility.
*Advised by Shobith 2026-09-17, accepted. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**Curriculum as a short handbook.** First principles, one linear loop: take a problem, validate,
build, validate again, iterate. Weekly reviews to unblock anyone going directionally wrong. If the
cohort lacks the basics, spend the first two months on case studies.
*Advised by Shobith 2026-09-17. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**Start small.** Five to ten companies worked closely beats a wide open cohort.
*Advised by Shobith 2026-09-17. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

## The parallel track

Companies that already have traction, taken on alongside the cohort. Less resource per company,
and more confidence in front of the next investor.

Thresholds: B2B, three to five pilots lined up. B2C, roughly 100 to 500 users. Traction, not
revenue.
*Proposed by Adithya 2026-09-17, agreed to run in parallel.
→ `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

## Publishing

**Record everything, filter by channel.** From inception to execution. What gets published is
sorted afterwards rather than decided in advance.

Adithya's framing: startups get covered at launch and then nobody knows what happened. It takes
someone investigating years later. Publish the whole arc, including the incubator itself.

Ethan proposed separate accounts by content type with a low-frequency portfolio account for
startups. Arjun pushed back on the maintenance burden. Deferred rather than settled.
*Agreed in principle 2026-09-24. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-24.md`*
