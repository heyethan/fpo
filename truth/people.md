# People

Contains each founder's own assessment of their record, written by them. Founders only until they
each say otherwise.

## The four

**Ethan Kiran Dsouza.** In the startup ecosystem since 2021. Has failed at startups, built
successful ones, and helped other people set up companies. Eight months of R&D on FPO talking to
stakeholders. Positions himself between expert and student, because his own bar for expert is
high. On the ground in Mangalore through to the end of the year; the other three are travelling.

**Arjun.** Tech research including multi-concurrency SSR. Has trained students in small and large
cohorts for placements. Runs a digital services company with real customers. Answers expert, and
the reason is specific: "I know my roadmap, I can generalise it for students who will walk it."
Was in InUnity's cohort, which is why his differentiation question carries weight.

**Adithya Prakash Nayak.** Chronos in 2017, customised apparel. Farak from 2018 to 2021, a youth
consortium that ran two conferences of 10,000-plus attendees. Head of Legal and Operations at
Effinity. Co-headed brand and go-to-market at Rapture Innovation Labs, which raised INR 91 lakh on
Indiegogo. Sold and leased property at 17. Has taught 450-plus CA and CS students since 2020.
Internships across PE and VC teams, and now structures SPVs, foundations and trusts in ADGM.

Answers student, and explains why: "I have started four things and none of them is still trading.
I know every mistake in the first eighteen months because I have made most of them." His own
summary: "I start things well and I have not yet carried one past the point where enthusiasm runs
out. That is the exact failure FPO is meant to solve for other people, which is either the best
reason to trust me on it or the worst."

**Aneesh M Bhat.** Has watched founding teams fail up close, which is where his conviction about
choosing the initial team comes from. CTO mistakes including production incidents. Compliance and
operations from running his own business. Prefers bootstrapping. Confident end to end on software
and on business taste; names investment structuring, compliance and tax, and brand positioning as
his gaps.
