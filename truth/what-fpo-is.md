# What FPO is

FPO is a codename. The company has no name yet. Naming happens at the end of the branding
workshop, not before.

## The one-line version

FPO takes a small, selected group of people in Mangalore and stays with them until they have a
real business or a real career, and it earns from the companies and funds that want access to
them.

*Assembled from the intake and both meetings. Not yet signed off by all four as a single sentence.
→ `archive/exercises/intake-synthesis.md`*

## Settled

**Commercial, not a nonprofit.** Ethan's intake answer proposed a nonprofit. The other three
described paying customers and equity. Resolved in the room: "It would be an FPO, as the group
name suggests."
*Settled 2026-09-17, all four. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**Two programmes running in parallel, not one integrated programme.**
*Settled 2026-09-17, all four. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**Stage: prototyping.** Two founders had said building, two had said idea. Ethan called it
semantics, the group converged, and Arjun landed the word.
*Settled 2026-09-17, all four. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

**The problem, in one line: creating efficient and employable youth.** Four intake answers about
the problem collapsed into this. Flip it and you have the problem statement.
*Settled 2026-09-24, proposed by Adithya, accepted by the group.
→ `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-24.md`*

**Research is a side effect, not an outcome.** "It's a tree. It's a branch." No dissent.
*Settled 2026-09-24, all four. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-24.md`*

## The three-tier outcome

Not everyone in a cohort becomes a founder, and that is by design rather than by disappointment.

1. **Entrepreneur.** Best case.
2. **Researcher.** Strong technically, not built for scaling a business.
3. **Skilled workforce.** Neither, but employable and good at the work.

"You join where you think you belong, we'll throw you where we think you belong."

This is what reconciles the founder-versus-talent tension from the intake. Both outcomes are wins.
*Framed by Arjun 2026-09-17, agreed by the group. → `archive/meetings/meeting-2026-09-17.md`*

## What FPO is not

Three of four founders independently rejected the same versions of FPO: a place to get money, a
credit-point scheme for colleges, a certificate programme posing as incubation, and an investor
that puts money first. Arjun's separate objection was to FPO becoming a cult.
*From the intake, question D3. → `archive/exercises/intake-synthesis.md`*
