FPO · Exercise One · Brand Journey Framework

What counts as FPO succeeding

Four founders answered the same four questions independently. Their outcomes agree on direction. They part company on whether FPO getting paid is part of winning, and on how precisely the question was taken.

The vertical axis is not a disagreement. Three founders answered the question directionally and one answered it in thresholds, which is a difference in how the question was taken, not in what anyone wants. Read the horizontal axis as the real split.

Question two

One answer, three placeholders

Asked what FPO must be known for, three founders named a category of activity rather than a reputation.

  • Ethan: works with startups, founders and talent, helping them grow
  • Aneesh: a place that facilitates and supports talent
  • Arjun: quality resources, tinkering mindset

None separates FPO from InUnity or DK24. Adithya's "for being reliable" is the only reputation on the page, and the only one with a method attached.

Question three

Publish what, exactly

Three founders independently chose publishing as the mechanism. What gets published is the opposite thing.

  • Aneesh publishes the wins: PR around the first cohort
  • Adithya publishes the failures: outcomes at 12, 24 and 36 months, people who left, predictions checked against what happened, rejections explained

Same word, opposite strategy. Round one hid agreement behind different words. This hides disagreement behind the same one.

To settle

Three open

  • Does FPO getting paid count as success, or only people improving
  • Publish wins or publish failures
  • Is research an outcome or a side effect

The first one blocks question two. A reputation cannot be chosen until you agree what it is supposed to produce.
