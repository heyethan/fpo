# FPO — Exercise Three: The Two-Column Contrarian Exercise

**Source:** Ralston, *How to Rebrand Yourself Online* ch7 (Common Enemy / The Differentiation
Exercise); *Disgustingly Good Personal Brand* ch5; *Personal Brand Stand Out* ch5-6.
Supporting: Harry Dry on conflict, draw a line down the page and write two opposites.

**For:** all four founders, answered independently.
**Time:** 30 minutes. This is the slow one.

## Scope decision

Rows are about **incubators, accelerators and cohort programmes** specifically, not about Mangalore
or tier-2 talent generally. Narrow produces sharper rows. The wide version is what produced prose
instead of rows in the intake.

## Adaptation note

Ralston's five-row minimum is a test of one person's depth in their industry: "if you can't get to
five, that's a signal, not a formatting problem." FPO's decision is five per founder, because the
depth test only works per person. The intake asked for three, which was the wrong number, and three
of four wrote prose instead of rows anyway.

## Form text

**Q1.** Five rows. Left: something people running incubators, accelerators or cohort programmes say
or do that you think is wrong. Right: what you believe instead.

> Format each row as: THEY SAY ... / WE SAY ...
>
> One you already have, from the last round: they say a bigger cohort means a better programme. We
> say an incubator that takes everyone cannot vouch for anyone. That is row one. Write four more.
>
> It has to be a real disagreement. The test is whether someone running an incubator would argue
> with you about it. If every programme in Mangalore could sign their name under your sentence, it
> is not a position.
>
> If you cannot reach five, write however many are true and say where you got stuck.

**Q2.** Of your five, which one would start the biggest argument in a room full of people who run
these programmes?

**Q3.** Which one could you defend with something you have actually done or watched happen? Name
the thing.

**Q4.** Which row do you expect the other three to disagree with you on?
