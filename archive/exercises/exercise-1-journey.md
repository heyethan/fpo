# FPO — Exercise One: Brand Journey Framework

**Source:** Caleb Ralston, *Disgustingly Good Personal Brand* ch1; *How to Start a Personal
Brand* ch2.

**For:** all four founders, answered independently. Do not discuss first.
**Time:** 20 to 30 minutes.

Four questions, answered in the order given, starting from the end and working backward. That
direction is the point. You start with the outcome you want and derive everything else from it.

Two rules, same as last time:
1. **Answer alone.** If you sync up first, I get one answer four times and learn nothing.
2. **Specific beats impressive.** If it can't be measured or checked, rewrite it.

---

**Q1.** What is FPO's desired outcome? What has to be true in three years for you to say this
worked?

> Your intake answer to "why are you doing this" was about you. This one is about FPO. If they're
> the same for you, say so.

**Q2.** For that outcome to happen, what would FPO have to be known for? Name the one reputation
that produces your Q1 answer.

**Q3.** What does FPO have to do to earn that reputation? You get known for things by doing them
and then talking about what you did. Give concrete actions.

**Q4.** Inside that list, what do you not yet know how to do? Name the gaps honestly. This is the
question that turns the rest into a roadmap.

---

**Q5.** Which of the four was hardest to write, and why?
